Bondfall privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026

Bondfall is a puzzle game by Kevin Ramdath. This policy covers the Bondfall app on Android, iOS and the web.

What we collect

Nothing. Bondfall has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Your best score and the list of molecules you have discovered are saved on your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Game updates

When the app starts, it checks Expo's update service (expo.dev) for a newer version of the game's content. That request carries technical details needed to deliver the update, such as the app version, platform and update channel. It does not include your name, contacts, location or anything you do in the game. Expo's own privacy policy is at expo.dev/privacy.

Children

Bondfall does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
